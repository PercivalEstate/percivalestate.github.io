Save Tompion Community Hall

Tompion Community Hall is at risk of being permanently converted into Council offices. If you live on or near the Percival Estate, you can object — it takes about a minute.

Planning application P2026/1577/FUL
Islington Council — change of use of Tompion Community Hall, 40 Percival Street, London EC1V 0EB, from community use to office use.

  • Send your objection

Why this matters

Tompion Community Hall is the community facility on the Percival Estate, on the ground floor of Grimthorpe House at 40 Percival Street. Islington Council has submitted planning application P2026/1577/FUL, which would change the Hall permanently from community use to office use.

The Percival Estate Community objects to the application. Objections from individual residents matter in their own right, because they are direct evidence of current demand for a community facility on the estate — something no one but residents can give.

You do not need to know anything about planning to object. There is no form to get wrong and no jargon to learn. What counts is that you say who you are, where you live, and why the Hall matters to you — in your own words. A short objection written by you is worth more than a long one copied from somebody else.

Why we are objecting

  • Tompion Community Hall was provided as a community facility for the estate, and the application proposes to change that permanently to office use.
  • There is current resident interest in bringing the Hall back into community use.
  • Residents have not had normal access to the Hall for substantial periods since 2019, so a lack of recent bookings does not show a lack of demand.
  • Facilities elsewhere in the borough are welcome, but they are not necessarily equivalent to having a community facility on the estate itself.
  • We do not believe the application has adequately demonstrated that Tompion Community Hall is no longer needed.

How it works

  1. Answer a few short questions below.
  2. We put together an objection for you, which you can read and change.
  3. Press the button to open your own email app with it ready to send — or copy it and send it however you prefer.

Send your objection

Your details are used only in your browser to prepare your objection. The Percival Estate Community does not receive or store the information you enter here.

Your objection email itself is a different matter, and you should know before you send it: it is addressed to Islington Council and copies us at tra@percivalestate.com, so that we can keep count of how many residents have objected. If you would rather we did not see it, delete that address in your email app before pressing Send — your objection to the Council counts just the same.

Not sure what to write? Some ideas

You could mention:

  • that you would attend residents meetings there
  • that you would attend community events
  • that having a facility within the estate is important to you
  • that you would use activities or classes there
  • that you want a shared place where neighbours can meet
  • why travelling to another community centre is not equivalent for you

These are prompts only — nothing here is added to your objection. Please write it your own way.
