More about the campaign

Why this matters

Tompion Community Hall is the only community space on our estate. It is the single-storey building at 42 Percival Street.

The Council has asked for permission to turn it into offices for its parking team. If that is granted, the hall is gone for good.

The Council has to take account of objections from residents. Yours counts even if it is short. You do not need to know anything about planning. Say who you are, where you live, and why the hall matters to you.

What the Council says

In its own application, the Council says:

  • the hall shut in June 2019 for building work
  • it decided in 2021 not to open it again as a community centre
  • there are other halls within a 15 minute walk
  • so the hall is not needed any more
Why we disagree

Every point here comes from the Council’s own application, so you do not have to take our word for it.

  • We did not stop using the hall. It was shut. The Council’s own report says it “has not been available for community bookings” since 2019. No bookings does not mean nobody wants it.
  • The Council used it as an office itself. Its own staff worked there from 2021 until November 2024. It has only been empty since then.
  • The report says a resident asked for it back. It then says other halls will do. Every objection is more proof that people want ours.
  • A 15 minute walk is a long way. It is a long way with a pushchair, or a walking stick, or three children. This is the only hall on our estate.
  • The lease runs to at least 2034. There is no rush to give the hall up.
  • It is still our polling station. We vote there.
