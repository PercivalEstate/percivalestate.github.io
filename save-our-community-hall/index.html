Save Tompion Community Hall

Tompion Community Hall is at risk of being permanently converted into Council offices. If you live on or near the Percival Estate, you can object — it takes about a minute.

Planning application P2026/1577/FUL
Islington Council — change of use of Tompion Hall, 42 Percival Street, London EC1V 0EB, from community use (Class F1) to offices (Class E(g)) for the Council’s Parking Service.

  • Send your objection

Why this matters

Tompion Hall is the community facility on the Percival Estate: a single-storey building of about 208 square metres at 42 Percival Street, built as part of the estate by the Guinness Trust and leased to Islington Council for community use since 2009. The Council has now applied, under P2026/1577/FUL, to change it permanently into offices for its Parking Service.

The Percival Estate Community objects to the application. Objections from individual residents matter in their own right, because they are direct evidence of current demand for a community facility on the estate — something no one but residents can give.

You do not need to know anything about planning to object. There is no form to get wrong and no jargon to learn. What counts is that you say who you are, where you live, and why the Hall matters to you — in your own words. A short objection written by you is worth more than a long one copied from somebody else.

What the application says

It is worth knowing the Council’s own case before you write, and it is all in the documents on the planning register. In short, the Council says the Hall closed in June 2019 for refurbishment works, that it decided in 2021 not to reopen it as a community centre, that the Housing Revenue Account declared it surplus in July 2025, and that there are enough other community venues within a fifteen-minute walk. On that basis it says the Hall is no longer needed and the Parking Service should have it instead.

Those documents also contain the answer. Read the application and its documents on Islington’s planning register if you would like to judge for yourself.

Why we are objecting

Every point below comes from the Council’s own submission, so none of it depends on taking our word for anything.

  • Residents did not stop using the Hall — it was closed to them. The Community Needs Assessment says the Hall closed in 2019 for refurbishment and “has not been available for community bookings” since. Six years of no bookings is the result of that decision, not evidence that nobody wants it.
  • The Council has been using it as an office already. The same document records that Council Estate Services occupied the Hall as an office base from 2021/22 until November 2024. It has stood genuinely empty only since then.
  • The application records resident interest, then sets it aside. The Community Needs Assessment notes “some interest from a local resident to return the site to community use” and answers it by pointing at other venues. Objections now are the evidence of demand it says does not exist.
  • Nearby is not the same as on the estate. The seven alternatives listed are a five to fifteen minute walk away. That is a real distance for an older resident, a parent with a pushchair or anyone who finds getting about difficult, and Tompion Hall is the only community facility on the Percival Estate itself.
  • The lease has years left to run. The Council’s own documents say it holds the lease from the Guinness Trust until at least 2034. Changing the use is a permanent decision taken well before that lease runs out.
  • The Hall is still a polling station. The application confirms it has continued in that civic use throughout, which does not sit easily alongside a permanent parking-enforcement office.

We do not believe the application has adequately demonstrated that Tompion Hall is no longer needed. It is for the Council to show that, and residents saying otherwise is the most direct evidence there is.

How it works

  3. Press the button to open your own email app with it ready to send — or copy it and send it however you prefer.

Send your objection

Your details are used only in your browser to prepare your objection. The Percival Estate Community does not receive or store the information you enter here.

Your objection email itself is a different matter, and you should know before you send it: it is addressed to Islington Council and copies us at tra@percivalestate.com, so that we can keep count of how many residents have objected. If you would rather we did not see it, delete that address in your email app before pressing Send — your objection to the Council counts just the same.

Not sure what to write? Some ideas

You could mention:

  • that you would attend residents meetings there
  • that you would attend community events
  • that having a facility within the estate is important to you
  • that you would use activities or classes there
  • that you want a shared place where neighbours can meet
  • why travelling to another community centre is not equivalent for you

These are prompts only — nothing here is added to your objection. Please write it your own way.
